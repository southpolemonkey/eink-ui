Ink UI Kit

A one-colour component kit for apps that talk to e-paper displays. Pure black on warm off-white, a heavy 2px border as the signature, pill controls, and a panel component that renders like a 1-bit screen. Drop in ink-card-style-kit.css and use the class names shown below.

1

Colors

There is no accent hue. Hierarchy comes entirely from value: black for action and emphasis, three greys for text, two for lines, one off-white ground.
